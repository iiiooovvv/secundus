Before You Go

Create Your Barbarian

Roll, choose, or make your own. The waste doesn't care where you came from. Your equipment defines what you can do. Build the model. The model is the character sheet.

Equipment (d6)

Roll a d6, pick one, or kit-bash your own. Each piece of equipment grants a keyword skill as long as you carry it.

d6EquipmentGrantsEffect
1RifleMarksmanRanged. Roll two dice on Targeting. Read the highest.
2MaulBrawlerMelee. Roll one extra die in the Encounter. Count the sixes.
3BackpackSurvivorOnce per expedition, ignore one Wound result from the Doom Pool.
4CloakCamouflageThreats ignore this barbarian unless they are the nearest target.
5StaffWeirdOnce per round, reroll one die of your choice. Take the new result.
6—ScoutRoll two dice on Explore. Move the sum of both.

Weapons (d6)

Roll a d6, pick one, or invent your own. Your weapon is separate from your equipment.

d6WeaponEffect
1Pipe MaulMelee. Weapon advantage in the Encounter Roll.
2Salvage BowRanged. Shoot before threats advance. No melee advantage.
3Chain BladeMelee. Weapon advantage. On a 6, all threats in contact take a hit.
4Stub PistolRanged. Shoot and count the highest die. Can also fight in melee, no advantage.
5Scrap SpearMelee. Weapon advantage. May fight threats up to 3" away.
6Relic WeaponRanged or Melee — choose each round. Weapon advantage always. Roll an extra doom die when used.

Armor

Take it or leave it. Armor lets you reroll one die from a Doom Pool Climax roll once per expedition. It costs nothing. It weighs everything.
