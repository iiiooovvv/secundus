Before You Go

Create Your Barbarian

Roll, choose, or make your own. Your barbarian starts with two artifacts — one weapon, one equipment, or two of either. Each artifact grants a keyword skill for as long as you carry it. Lose the artifact, lose the skill. The model is the character sheet.

Pick or roll twice. No duplicates. At least one must be a weapon. Keyword Skills Reference →
