Before You Go

Create Your Barbarian

Roll, choose, or make your own. Your barbarian starts with two artifacts — weapons or equipment, whatever the waste left behind. Each artifact grants a keyword skill for as long as you carry it. Lose the artifact, lose the skill.

Pick or roll twice on the artifact table. No duplicates. Keyword Skills Reference →

Armor

Take it or leave it. Armor lets you reroll one die from a Doom Pool Climax roll once per expedition. It costs nothing. It weighs everything.
