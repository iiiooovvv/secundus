Before You Go

Create Your Barbarian

Roll, choose, or make your own. The waste doesn't care where you came from. One skill. One weapon. Armor or don't. Name them something you won't regret.

Skills (d6)

Roll a d6, pick one, or invent your own.

d6SkillEffect
1ScoutRoll two dice on Explore. Move the sum of both.
2MarksmanRoll two dice when shooting. Read the highest.
3BrawlerRoll one extra die in melee. Count the sixes.
4SurvivorOnce per expedition, ignore one Wound result from the Doom Pool.
5CamouflageThreats ignore this barbarian unless they are the nearest target.
6WeirdOnce per round, reroll one die of your choice. Take the new result.

Weapons (d6)

Roll a d6, pick one, or invent your own.

d6WeaponEffect
1Pipe MaulMelee. Weapon advantage in the Encounter Roll.
2Salvage BowRanged. Shoot before threats advance. No melee advantage.
3Chain BladeMelee. Weapon advantage. On a 6, all threats in contact take a hit.
4Stub PistolRanged. Shoot and count the highest die. Can also fight in melee, no advantage.
5Scrap SpearMelee. Weapon advantage. May fight threats up to 3" away.
6Relic WeaponRanged or Melee — choose each round. Weapon advantage always. Roll an extra doom die when used.

Armor

Take it or leave it. Armor lets you reroll one die from a Doom Pool Climax roll once per expedition. It costs nothing. It weighs everything.

Name

Roll three times — once per table. Combine. That's who you are now.
