How to Play

A Round of Secundus

Each round runs five phases in order. Every phase feeds the next. The doom is always building. This is what one round looks like.

Before the First Round

Setup

Build your barbarian. Choose two artifacts — weapons or equipment. Each artifact grants a keyword skill for as long as you carry it. Place your barbarian at one edge of the board and set your expedition goal — discover three sites, retrieve a relic, survive six rounds, kill six threats. Something to aim for. Something the waste will try to take from you.

Set aside the doom dice, one color apart from the rest. They mean something different. Put your barbarian at the edge. Look out at what's ahead. You begin alone.

Each barbarian in base contact with a threat

You fight whatever reached you. Gather dice: one for a melee weapon or Brawler skill, one per Wound already marked, one doom die per threat in contact. Count the sixes. Each six kills one threat. Every doom die rolled joins the Pool.

The Doom Pool

Starts empty. Only grows. Fed by Explore, Targeting, and Encounter rolls whenever you choose doom dice — or when threats force them. Drives Threat Pursuit. Triggers wounds and spawns at Climax. Clears after Phase V. Starts filling again immediately.

Wounds

Each 1 in the Climax Roll marks a Wound. Each Wound also adds a die to future Encounter Rolls — the corruption makes you more dangerous as it destroys you. At 5 Wounds, choose your end. At 6, the waste chooses for you.

Sites & Threats

Spawned by Explore and Climax rolls. Sites persist until claimed by contact. Threats activate on Phase III. Roll to determine what type each is when it spawns.

Site Roll → Threat Type Roll →

Winning & Losing

Set your expedition goal before you begin. Discover three sites and escape. Retrieve a relic. Survive six rounds. Kill six threats. Victory means achieving it with at least one barbarian off the board. Defeat means the waste took them all. A pyrrhic victory is possible — and whether it counts is a question Secundus will not answer for you.

Between Expeditions

Survivors carry their scars. Clear Wounds back to 1 marked. Spend finds for better equipment. Gain another skill. Then go back in. They go back because staying is worse.
