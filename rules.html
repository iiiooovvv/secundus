Setup

Create your barbarian. Choose one skill. Choose one weapon. Choose armor or don't. Place your barbarian at one edge of the board and set your expedition goal — discover three sites, retrieve a relic, survive six rounds, kill six threats. Something to aim for. Something the waste will try to take from you. Set aside the doom dice, one color set apart from the rest. They mean something different. Put your barbarian at the edge and look out at what's ahead. You begin alone.

How to Play

Rules & Mechanics

The Round

Phase I — Explore

Each barbarian moves and discovers. What they find depends on what they rolled and how much doom they fed into the ground to get there.

Explore Roll →

Phase II — Targeting

Barbarians with line of sight and a ranged weapon may shoot before threats advance. One roll, one result. Then the threats move.

Targeting Roll →

Phase III — Threat Pursuit

Threats don't roll to move. The Doom Pool moves them. The heavier the pool, the faster everything closes in.

Threat Pursuit →

Phase IV — Encounter

Whatever reached base contact, you fight. Wounds make you more dangerous. Doom dice make the pool heavier. Count the sixes.

Encounter Roll →

Phase V — Climax

The doom comes due. Roll everything in the Pool. Ones wound. The highest spawns something new. Then the pool clears and starts filling again.

Climax Roll →

The Doom Pool

The Doom Pool is the heartbeat of Secundus. It starts empty. It only grows. Every explore roll, every shot, every melee feeds it. It doesn't punish you immediately — it waits. A patient thing. The reckoning comes at Phase V, every round, without fail.

Wounds

Each 1 in the Climax Roll marks a Wound on a barbarian. Wounds accumulate. At five, you choose your end. At six, you're gone — monster, fugitive, or corpse. The waste decides which.

Wound Threshold →

Winning & Losing

Set your goal before you begin. Discover three sites and escape. Retrieve a relic and get out. Survive six rounds. Kill six threats. Victory means achieving it with at least one barbarian off the board. Defeat means the waste took them all. Between those two things is a pyrrhic victory — and whether that counts as winning is a question Secundus will not answer for you.

Between Expeditions

Barbarians who escape carry their scars forward. Clear their Wounds back to one marked. Spend what they found for better equipment. Give them another skill — they've earned it, whatever earning means now. They go back in. They go back because the alternative is to stay and let the world finish dying around them.
