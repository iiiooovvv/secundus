Setup

Create your barbarian. Choose one skill. Choose one weapon. Choose armor or don't. Place your barbarian at one edge of the board and set your expedition goal — discover three sites, retrieve a relic, survive six rounds, kill six threats. Something to aim for. Something the waste will try to take from you. Set aside the doom dice, one color set apart from the rest. They mean something different. Put your barbarian at the edge and look out at what's ahead. You begin alone.

Before You Go

Create Your Barbarian

Roll, choose, or make your own. The waste doesn't care where you came from. One skill. One weapon. Armor or don't. Name them something you won't regret.

Armor

Take it or leave it. Armor lets you reroll one die from a Doom Pool Climax roll once per expedition. It costs nothing. It weighs everything.

How to Play

Rules & Mechanics

The Round

Phase I — Explore

Each barbarian makes an Explore Roll. Roll dice — one always, more if you have the skill for it, and a doom die if you want to press deeper into dangerous ground. Move a number of inches equal to the sum. The highest die tells you what you found: 1–3 you've drawn something toward you, 4–5 you found a site and something found you, 6 a clean discovery. Every doom die you roll goes into the Doom Pool. You cannot take it back.

Phase II — Targeting & Encounter

Barbarians with line of sight may shoot. Roll dice, read the highest — miss, hit, or critical. Then threats advance six inches toward the nearest barbarian and you fight what reaches you. Gather dice for the Encounter Roll: one if you have the Brawler skill or weapon advantage, plus one for each Wound already marked — the corruption grants dark strength — plus doom dice for each threat in contact. Count the sixes. Each six kills one threat if you carry a melee weapon. Every doom die rolled joins the Pool.

Phase III — Climax

The doom comes due. Roll everything in the Pool. For each die showing a 1, a barbarian marks a Wound and you describe how the cursed land warps them. The corruption spreading. Spreading through vein and bone. The highest result spawns a new threat — folk on 1–3, beasts on 4–5, a monster on 6. Then the Pool clears and the round begins again and the Pool will fill again and it always fills faster than it emptied.

The Doom Pool

The Doom Pool is the heartbeat of Secundus. It starts empty. It only grows. Every explore roll, every shot, every melee feeds it. The pool doesn't punish you immediately — it waits. A patient thing. Pressure builds round by round and the reckoning comes and every die that showed a 1 is a Wound and every Wound is a mark and the marks accumulate and at five Wounds you choose — one last desperate action and then lost, or flee and survive as something changed. At six Wounds the barbarian is gone. Become a monster under threat control. Flee into the wilds. Collapse where they stood. The waste chooses which.

Winning & Losing

Set your goal before you begin. Discover three sites and escape. Retrieve a relic and get out. Survive six rounds. Kill six threats. Something to aim for. Victory means achieving it with at least one barbarian off the board. Defeat means the waste took them all. Between those two things is a pyrrhic victory — goal achieved, more than half the warband lost — and whether that counts as winning is a question Secundus will not answer for you.

Between Expeditions

Barbarians who escape carry their scars forward. Clear their Wounds back to one marked. Spend what they found for better equipment. Give them another skill — they've earned it, whatever earning means now. They go back in. They go back because the alternative is to stay and let the world finish dying around them. Some came through. Some were men once. Doesn't matter which anymore.
