Encyclopedia

The Rolls

Every roll in Secundus. What dice to pick up. What to read. What it costs you.

Phase I

Explore Roll

Once per barbarian, each round.
Gather Dice
  • Always 1 standard die
  • Scout skill +1 standard die
  • Unarmored +1 standard die
  • Optional Any number of doom dice — each one goes into the Pool
Roll & Read

First you may move a number of inches equal to the sum of all dice. Then read the highest single die.

Highest DieWhat You Found
1–3Something found you. Spawn one threat.
4–5You found something. It found you too. Spawn one site and one threat.
6Clean. Spawn one site only.
Every doom die rolled — whether you chose to roll it or not — goes into the Doom Pool. You cannot take it back. Spawned threats are resolved with a Threat Type Roll. Spawned sites are resolved with a Site Roll.
Phase II

Targeting Roll

Once per barbarian with a ranged weapon and line of sight, each round.
Gather Dice
  • Always 1 standard die
  • Marksman skill +1 standard die
  • Doom +1 doom die per threat with ranged weapon in LOS.
Roll & Read

Read the highest single die.

Highest DieResult
1–2Miss. Nothing.
3–5Hit. Threat is eliminated (Folk and Beasts) or marked (Monster).
6Critical. Threat eliminated regardless of type.
Barbarians with melee-only weapons do not make a Targeting Roll. They wait for the Encounter Roll.
Phase IV

Encounter Roll

Once per barbarian in base contact with a threat, each round.
Gather Dice
  • Always 1 die if you have a melee weapon or the Brawler skill
  • Wounds +1 die per Wound already marked — the corruption gives dark strength
  • Doom +1 doom die per threat in base contact — each goes into the Pool
Roll & Read

Count the sixes. Each six eliminates one threat in contact, if you carry a melee weapon.

ResultEffect
Any 6One threat eliminated per 6 rolled (Folk and Beasts).
Any 6 vs MonsterMonster is only eliminated on a 6 — otherwise it survives.
No 6sNothing. The threats remain. The Pool keeps what it collected.
Every doom die rolled in this phase joins the Pool — which is rolled in full at the Climax Roll. If you have no melee weapon and no Brawler skill, you gather no dice. You take the contact. Wounds accumulated here affect your Wound Threshold.
Phase V

Climax Roll

Once per round. The doom comes due.
Gather Dice
  • Doom Pool Every die currently in the Doom Pool
  • Armor If your barbarian wears armor, set one Pool die aside before rolling — once per expedition
Roll & Read

Read every die. Then read the highest single die.

ResultEffect
Each 1 rolledOne barbarian marks a Wound. Describe how the land warps them.
Highest die 1–3Spawn one Folk threat.
Highest die 4–5Spawn one Beast threat.
Highest die 6Spawn one Monster threat.
Each 1 marked is a Wound — see Wound Threshold. Each threat spawned is resolved with a Threat Type Roll. After the Climax Roll, the Pool clears. The next round it fills again through the Explore and Encounter rolls. It always fills faster than it emptied.
Wounds

Wound Threshold

Tracked across rounds. No roll — a reckoning.
Track & Read

Each barbarian tracks Wounds marked from Climax rolls. Wounds are not healed between rounds — only between expeditions (cleared to 1 marked).

Wounds MarkedEffect
1–4The barbarian continues. Scarred but standing. Each Wound adds a die to the Encounter Roll.
5Choose: one last desperate action, then lost — or flee the board and survive as something changed.
6Gone. Become a monster under threat control. Flee into the wilds. Collapse where they stood. The waste chooses which.
Wounds are dealt by the Climax Roll. Each Wound also adds a die to the Encounter Roll — the corruption makes you more dangerous as it kills you. A Sanctuary Site clears 1 Wound. That is all the mercy this game offers.
Phase III

Threat Pursuit

Once per round. The threats move.
No Roll — The Pool Decides

Threats do not roll to move. They move a number of inches equal to the sum of all dice currently in the Doom Pool, toward the nearest barbarian.

ConditionEffect
Pool emptyThreats do not move. They wait.
Pool has diceEach threat moves inches equal to the Pool sum toward the nearest barbarian.
Camouflage skillThat barbarian is ignored unless they are the nearest target.
Reach base contactThreat stays in contact. Resolved in Phase IV.
A large Pool does not just wound you at the Climax Roll — it moves every threat toward you first. Threats that reach base contact are resolved in the Encounter Roll. The doom has weight in every phase.
Discovery

Site Roll

When a site is spawned by an Explore Roll.
Gather Dice
  • Always 1 standard die
Roll & Read

Place a site marker. Roll to determine what it is. A barbarian must move into contact to claim it.

RollSiteBenefit
1Barbarian CampRecruit one new barbarian. They arrive with 1 Wound already marked.
2–3Ancient CacheTake one piece of equipment from the supply.
4–5Forgotten ShrineGain one skill. Roll or choose.
6SanctuaryClear 1 Wound. Safe zone — threats will not advance within 6".
Sites are spawned by the Explore Roll. They persist on the board until claimed or the expedition ends. A site contested by threats still requires contact to claim — get there first, or resolve it through the Encounter Roll.
Spawning

Threat Type Roll

When a threat is spawned — by Explore, Climax, or any other source.
Gather Dice
  • Always 1 standard die
Roll & Read

Place the threat at the nearest board edge. It activates next Phase III.

RollThreatBehavior
1–3FolkMay be ranged. Eliminated on any hit.
4–5BeastMelee only. Always advances. Eliminated on any hit.
6MonsterSpecial ability — define it when it spawns. Only eliminated on a 6.
When a Monster spawns, decide its special ability now — before it moves. Something it does that nothing else does. It activates next Threat Pursuit phase and can only be killed in the Encounter Roll on a 6.
