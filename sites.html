The Territory

Build Your Site

Sites are places. Ruins, margins, half-alive things. You make them the same way you make a barbarian — roll or choose, then place them on the board before the expedition begins.

Each site grants a keyword skill to any barbarian occupying it. Lose the site, lose the skill. A barbarian at a site is not just standing there. They are in relationship with it — and with everything else that is drawn to it.

Sites also carry alternate win conditions. These are not about killing. They are about the web of things around you: predators, scavengers, remnants, the ground itself. Win through the territory, not through the fight.

Roll once or pick. Place before play. A site occupies at least a 3″ footprint on the board. Keyword Skills Reference →

Occupation

A barbarian occupies a site if they end their activation within its boundary and no threat model is also within that boundary. Shared occupation does not count — the site is contested and neither model gains the skill.

Losing the Site

If a threat enters the site boundary, occupation ends immediately. The barbarian loses the granted skill until they re-establish occupation uncontested at the start of any future activation.

Multiple Barbarians

Two barbarians from the same warband can share a site without contest. Both gain the granted skill. Three or more barbarians occupying a single site must immediately make a Corruption Roll — the site is too small and the pressure too close.

Alternate Victory

Win Conditions

Standard victory is survival and extraction. Site win conditions are something else. They require a barbarian to be in a specific ecological relationship with other models on the board — not fighting them, but positioned relative to them in a way that means something.

Each site has one win condition. At the end of any round, check if it is met. If it is, the expedition ends immediately. That barbarian's player narrates the aftermath.

Collapsed Relay Tower Apex Watch

A barbarian occupies this site while at least three other models — threats or allies — are within 9″ and none of them are in base contact with each other.

The web holds. You are the centre of it. Everything around you is moving in relation to you without touching. That is a kind of power the waste does not usually allow.

Sump Hollow Still Water

A barbarian occupies this site for three consecutive rounds without making any attack or being the target of any attack.

Violence happened around you and you did not become part of it. In the hollow, beneath the sight lines, you waited. The waste passed over. You are still here.

Bone Cairn The Last Witness

A barbarian occupies this site while they are the only non-threat model remaining on the board, and at least two threat models are still alive.

Everyone else is gone and the threats are still circling. You are standing at the cairn. That is enough. It has always been enough. The cairn knows what you did to get here.

Scrap Depot Full Hands

A barbarian occupies this site while carrying two artifacts, and at least one other barbarian in the warband is carrying zero artifacts.

You took more than you needed. Someone else has nothing. The depot made that happen — it concentrated what little there is. That is the economy of this place.

Pressure Vent Controlled Burn

A barbarian occupies this site immediately after a threat model is eliminated within 6″ of it by any cause — including hazard effects from the vent itself.

The vent did the work or you used it as a weapon. Either way, something died close to a place that was already killing. You steered that. You made the hazard useful.

Old Road Junction Free Passage

A barbarian occupies this site while every other barbarian in the warband has already exited the board via any extraction point.

You stayed at the junction until the others were clear. You held the road open. The junction is yours now — not to keep, but because you were the last one through it and you chose to be.
