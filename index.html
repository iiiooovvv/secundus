Overview

What is Secundus?

The earth in its dying and men done it. Found a door in the wires and opened it and called what lay beyond Secundus like naming a cancer makes it tame. It isn't tame. The corruption bleeds through and marks the land and the land marks everything that walks it. Terminal Scavenger is a solo and cooperative miniature wargame for one to six players, commanding techno-barbarians through a dying world. You push deeper into the waste. The waste pushes back.

Each expedition sends your barbarians into corrupted territory. Shrines still hot with old power. Caches sealed before the fall. Sanctuaries the corruption hasn't reached yet. You explore, you fight, you accumulate doom, and the doom comes due. The tension lives in the Doom Pool: every risk you take adds dice that will eventually roll against you. Press hard and move fast. Or move careful and slow. The waste finds you either way.

Secundus is a game about diminishing returns and desperate choices. Your barbarians grow stronger and more corrupted in the same breath. The land marks your skin and marks what's under it. What makes you harder to kill makes you less like what you were. You go because there is no choice. You go because staying is worse. You go because the alternative is to lay down and let the waste take you.
