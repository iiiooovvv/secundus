Overview

What is Secundus?

Secundus is a solo and cooperative miniature wargame for one to six players. A game about diminishing returns and desperate choices.

Each expedition sends your techno-barbarian warband into corrupted territory. They grow stronger and more corrupted in the same breath.

They go because staying is worse.

They go because the alternative is to lay down and let the waste take you where you stand.
