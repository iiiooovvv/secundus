Keyword Abilities

Skills

Skills are keyword abilities. A keyword is a word that replaces a longer rule. It works as long as the artifact that grants it is on the model. Lose the artifact, lose the skill. Find new equipment at a site, gain the skill immediately.

Skills can be referenced by other rules. A rule that says "if a barbarian has Marksman" means only a barbarian carrying a Rifle. The artifact is the proof.

Weapon Skills
Encounter Roll

Brawler

Granted by: Maul
When this barbarian makes an Encounter Roll, roll one additional standard die. Each 6 eliminates one threat in base contact.
The extra die stacks with all other sources — Wounds, doom dice. Brawler makes the pool larger. More dice means more chances at 6s, and more chances at doom.
Encounter Roll

Cleave

Granted by: Pipe Maul
When this barbarian rolls a 6 on an Encounter Roll, that result eliminates all Folk and Beasts in base contact, not just one.
Cleave only triggers on a 6. It does not apply to Monsters — a 6 against a Monster still only counts as one hit. If multiple 6s are rolled, each one triggers Cleave independently.
Targeting RollThreat Pursuit

Suppression

Granted by: Salvage Bow
When this barbarian makes a Targeting Roll, any threat targeted — hit or miss — does not advance during Threat Pursuit this round.
Suppression applies regardless of the Targeting result. Even a miss pins the target in place for that round. Only one threat can be targeted per Targeting Roll. Choose carefully — a suppressed threat is one that won't close the gap.
Encounter Roll

Rend

Granted by: Chain Blade
When this barbarian scores a hit on an Encounter Roll, place a Rend marker on the threat instead of eliminating it. A threat with a Rend marker is eliminated by any subsequent hit.
Rend does not kill outright — it marks. A marked Folk or Beast is still on the board until it takes a second hit from any source. A marked Monster still requires a 6 to eliminate, but a non-6 hit removes the marker and counts as the hit that would normally do nothing. Track markers with a token.
Targeting RollEncounter Roll

Sidearm

Granted by: Stub Pistol
This barbarian may make both a Targeting Roll in Phase II and an Encounter Roll in Phase IV in the same round, even if they are in base contact with a threat.
Most barbarians must choose — ranged or melee. Sidearm removes that restriction. The Targeting Roll still requires line of sight. The Encounter Roll still requires base contact. Both rolls happen in their normal phases. Doom dice from both go into the Pool.
Encounter Roll

Reach

Granted by: Scrap Spear
This barbarian may make an Encounter Roll against any threat within 3 inches, not just threats in base contact.
Reach extends the barbarian's melee range. A threat within 3" is a valid target even if not touching the base. The barbarian must still be within 3" at the start of Phase IV — Reach does not allow movement. Threats with Reach still advance normally in Phase III.
Equipment Skills
Targeting Roll

Marksman

Granted by: Rifle
When this barbarian makes a Targeting Roll, roll two standard dice. Read the highest single die.
Marksman only applies during Phase II. It does not grant an additional attack. It improves the chance of a high result on a single roll. A barbarian without a ranged weapon cannot use Marksman — they make no Targeting Roll at all.
Explore Roll

Scout

Granted by: Worn Boots
When this barbarian makes an Explore Roll, roll two standard dice instead of one. Move the sum of both dice in inches before reading the result.
Scout does not change what you find — only how far you go before you find it. Movement and discovery are resolved together. You move first, then read.
Climax Roll

Survivor

Granted by: Backpack
Once per expedition, when this barbarian would mark a Wound from a Climax Roll, ignore that Wound. It is not marked.
Survivor triggers once per expedition, not once per round. After it has been used, it cannot be used again until the next expedition. It applies only to Wounds from the Climax Roll — not from other sources. Track whether it has been spent.
Threat Pursuit

Camouflage

Granted by: Cloak
During Threat Pursuit, threats do not move toward this barbarian unless this barbarian is the nearest target to that threat.
Camouflage changes targeting priority, not threat movement. Threats still move during Phase III. They simply move toward whoever else is closest. If this barbarian is the only barbarian on the board, or if they are the nearest target, Camouflage has no effect and threats advance normally.

Weird

Granted by: Staff
Once per round, after any roll, this barbarian may reroll one die of their choice. Take the new result. The new result stands.
Weird can be used on any die — standard or doom — from any roll this barbarian is involved in. It cannot be used on dice rolled by other barbarians or by threats. The reroll is mandatory: once the die is picked up, the new result must be accepted. Weird resets at the start of each round.
Climax Roll

Resilient

Granted by: Flak Vest
Once per expedition, when this barbarian rolls a Climax Roll, they may reroll one die from the Doom Pool before reading results. Take the new result.
Resilient triggers once per expedition. Mark it when used — it does not reset between rounds. The reroll applies to one die only, chosen before any results are read. The new result cannot be rerolled again by any means in that same roll. Resilient changes outcomes, not Wounds already marked.
